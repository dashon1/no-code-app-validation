# Competitor Scan Worksheet

## Analyzing the Competitive Landscape to Find Your Position

Understanding competitors is essential for building a viable product. This worksheet guides you through systematically analyzing existing solutions, identifying gaps, and defining your differentiation strategy before you write a single line of code.

---

## Why Competitor Analysis Matters

Every market has competitors—even "new" categories have alternatives solving the same underlying problem through different means. Failing to understand the competitive landscape leads to expensive surprises: discovering your unique features already exist, pricing too high compared to alternatives, or building features nobody asked for while missing capabilities users desperately want.

Competitor analysis reveals:

- What already works (proven by customer adoption)
- What users complain about (opportunities for improvement)
- What nobody offers (potential differentiation)
- How to position your solution credibly
- What pricing the market accepts
