Idea Validation Checklist

Evaluate your app idea against these 15 critical success factors.

  • Does it solve a real problem?
    Your app must address an actual pain point that users experience regularly.
  • Is the market size sufficient?
    Ensure enough potential users exist to make your business viable.
  • Can users discover your app?
    You have a credible plan for reaching your target audience.
  • Can you reach users affordably?
    Customer acquisition costs should allow for profitable unit economics.
  • Will users pay for this?
    You have evidence of willingness to pay, not just interest.
  • Is the timing right?
    Market conditions, technology readiness, and trends support your idea.
  • Do you have a unfair advantage?
    Unique expertise, relationships, technology, or position gives you edge.
  • Can you build a MVP?
    You can create a minimum viable product with your available resources.
  • Is the problem recurring?
    Users face this problem regularly, not just once.
  • Do competitors have users?
    Existing solutions with customers confirm market demand.
  • Can you differentiate?
    You have a clear unique selling proposition vs. alternatives.
  • Is the market accessible?
    You can reach and serve your target customers with your resources.
  • Can you support customers?
    You can deliver and support your solution at your target price.
  • Do you understand regulations?
    You know and can comply with relevant legal requirements.
  • Can you scale eventually?
    The business model can grow beyond your initial capacity.

Buyer Intent Checklist

Verify that people are actively searching for solutions like yours.

  • Search volume exists
    People are searching for terms related to your solution (use Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest, or similar tools).
  • Communities discuss this problem
    Reddit, Facebook groups, forums, or Slack communities have active discussions about this pain point.
  • People are buying alternatives
    Existing solutions have paying customers, reviews, and testimonials.
  • You can reach the audience
    Your target users gather in accessible places (online or offline) where you can find them.
  • Direct feedback confirms demand
    You've talked to potential users who expressed interest or intent to purchase.
  • Social proof indicators present
    LinkedIn posts, tweets, or blog articles about this problem exist and get engagement.
