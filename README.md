## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Validate Your Idea (Tools 1-4)
Begin with the Idea Validation Checklist to get a baseline score. Move to Pain Point Scoring to understand user desperation. Use Competitor Scan to find market gaps. Complete Buyer Intent Checklist to confirm demand exists.

### Step 2: Plan Your Approach (Tools 5-7)
Generate landing page copy to test messaging. Use MVP Feature Filter to define scope. Run Pricing Test Worksheet to establish business viability.

### Step 3: Make Your Decision (Tool 8)
Complete the Go/No-Go Decision Scorecard with all your findings. Get your final recommendation and next steps.

### Step 4: Build Your PRD (Premium)
If you received a "Go" recommendation, use the PRD templates to document your requirements before development.
